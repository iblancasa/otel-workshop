A user reports that clicking **Buy** takes 8 seconds. CPU is fine, the
database looks normal, the server logs say `200 OK`. In this workshop you
find the bottleneck the way you would in production: with metrics, traces
and logs, not with `print()`.

Everything runs on your laptop in a [kind](https://kind.sigs.k8s.io/)
cluster. Nothing leaves your machine.

## This flavour

The shop, the steps and the bug are the same as in the otelite flavour. What
changes is where the OpenTelemetry Collector sends each signal, and which
tool you open to look at it:

| Signal | Tool | Open it at | Query language |
|--------|------|------------|----------------|
| traces | [Jaeger](https://www.jaegertracing.io/) v2 | [localhost:16686](http://localhost:16686) | search form |
| metrics | [Prometheus](https://prometheus.io/) 3 | [localhost:9090](http://localhost:9090) | PromQL |
| logs | [Loki](https://grafana.com/oss/loki/) 3 | through Perses | LogQL |
| dashboards | [Perses](https://perses.dev/) | [localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) | PromQL and LogQL in panels |

Four tools instead of one is the price of real tools: each one does its
signal properly. Jaeger, Prometheus and Perses are CNCF projects. Loki is
not, but it is what most people run next to them, it takes OTLP natively,
and Perses can query it. Five pods instead of two, about 1 GB more memory.
Pick the otelite flavour (switch at the top of the sidebar) if your laptop
has 8 GB or less.

## How the session goes

<Steps>

1. **At home, before the event.** [Prepare your laptop](/otel-workshop/cncf/prepare/).
   `make prepare` pulls and builds everything. Ten to fifteen minutes on
   good wifi.

2. **Talk** (20 min). What OpenTelemetry is. Traces, metrics, logs, the
   Collector. Your cluster idles with background traffic meanwhile.

3. **Hands-on** (70 min). Six short steps, left sidebar. Each page says
   what to run, what you should see, and why it matters.

</Steps>

## Reading these pages

Commands you type look like this:

```bash
make instrument
```

Terminal windows show the real output of each command, captured on the
speaker's laptop. They play once when they scroll into view and then stay,
so you can read them at your own pace. Your pod names and numbers will
differ.

<Aside type="tip" title="Tabs to keep open">
The shop: [localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080). Perses:
[localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000). Jaeger:
[localhost:16686](http://localhost:16686). Prometheus:
[localhost:9090](http://localhost:9090). All four work once `make prepare`
finishes.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Time ranges">
Jaeger searches the last hour by default, Prometheus graphs the last hour,
Perses dashboards the last 15 minutes with a 10 s refresh. When a view looks
empty, check its time picker before anything else.
</Aside>
