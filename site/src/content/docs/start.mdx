A user reports that clicking **Buy** takes 8 seconds. CPU is fine, the
database looks normal, the server logs say `200 OK`. In this workshop you
find the bottleneck the way you would in production: with metrics, traces
and logs, not with `print()`.

Everything runs on your laptop in a [kind](https://kind.sigs.k8s.io/)
cluster. Nothing leaves your machine.

## How the session goes

<Steps>

1. **At home, before the event.** [Prepare your laptop](/otel-workshop/prepare/).
   `make prepare` pulls and builds everything. Ten minutes on good wifi.

2. **Talk** (20 min). What OpenTelemetry is. Traces, metrics, logs, the
   Collector. Your cluster idles with background traffic meanwhile.

3. **Hands-on** (70 min). Six short steps, left sidebar. Each page says
   what to run, what you should see, and why it matters.

</Steps>

## The steps

| Step | What you do | Signal |
|------|-------------|--------|
| [0. The complaint](/otel-workshop/steps/00-complaint/) | Reproduce the 8 s button, look at the usual suspects, see an empty dashboard | none yet |
| [1. The stack](/otel-workshop/steps/01-stack/) | Understand the Collector and otelite you already deployed | |
| [2. Instrument](/otel-workshop/steps/02-instrument/) | Three env vars, three languages, zero code changes | all three appear |
| [3. Metric, trace, log](/otel-workshop/steps/03-investigate/) | Is it slow? Where? Why? | metric, trace, log |
| [4. Custom spans](/otel-workshop/steps/04-custom-spans/) | Make the invisible 7.7 s visible, with attributes and errors | trace |
| [5. Fix and prove](/otel-workshop/steps/05-fix/) | Apply the mitigation, prove it with all three signals | all three |
| [6. Service graph](/otel-workshop/steps/06-service-graph/) | Let the Collector draw the map: `service_graph` connector + otelite tab | metrics from traces |

If you are short on time: 0, 2, 3, 5.

## Reading these pages

Commands you type look like this:

```bash
make instrument
```

Terminal windows show the real output of each command, captured on the
speaker's laptop. They play once when they scroll into view and then stay,
so you can read them at your own pace. Your pod names and numbers will
differ.

<Aside type="tip" title="Two URLs to keep open">
The shop: [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080). The otelite
dashboard: [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000). Both work once
`make prepare` finishes.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="otelite freezes time">
Every otelite tab fixes its time window when the page loads. Tick
**Auto-refresh**, or click **Now** then **Apply**, to see new data. You will
forget this at least once.
</Aside>
